How to find the model number and the cartridge number

By Which Ink Fits · Last verified 16 Nov 2026

Read the model on the printer. Read the number on the box. Then open the matching page here and follow the link to the manufacturer.

On the printer

The model is the name on the front, such as DeskJet 2755e, PIXMA TR4720, EcoTank ET-2800, or MFC-L2710DW. Series names are shorter than the full model. If you only know the series, start at the series hub and then match the rest of the name.

On the cartridge or bottle

Ink cartridges show a number such as 67, PG-275, or 212. Toner shows a number such as TN-760 or 58A. Tank printers use a bottle such as GI-21 or 522, and the lead on those pages says so. High yield packs add XL or a similar mark. The color is printed next to the number.

Then check the source

Each page links the manufacturer document we used. Cartridge numbers differ by country. HP's pages quote the other columns when HP prints them. Sources and methods.

Frequently asked questions

Where is the printer model number?

On the front of the printer. HP, Canon, Epson, and Brother print the model there. If the front is worn, the same name is in the printer menu or on the setup sheet that shipped with it.

Where is the cartridge number?

On the cartridge, toner, or bottle, and again on the box. Match that number to the number on the model page, then confirm it on the manufacturer page linked there.

What if two pages disagree?

Use the manufacturer page. This guide quotes those pages and leaves a number off when the pages do not agree or do not print it.
