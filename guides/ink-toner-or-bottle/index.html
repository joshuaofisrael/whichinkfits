Ink cartridge, toner, or ink bottle: which does your printer use?

By Which Ink Fits · Last verified 16 Nov 2026

Look at the supply type, not the price. This site lists three kinds: ink cartridges, toner, and ink bottles. The printer's own name is usually the clue.

Ink cartridges

Inkjet printers hold a small sealed cartridge inside a carriage. Most HP DeskJet, OfficeJet, and ENVY models, most Canon PIXMA models that are not MegaTank, most Epson WorkForce and Expression models that are not EcoTank, and Brother's inkjet models such as the MFC-J4335DW are in this group.

Toner

Laser printers use a toner cartridge, not a liquid ink cartridge. HP's replacement document for the LaserJet Pro MFP M428 lists toner cartridge 58A and the high yield 58X. Brother's TN760 page names TN-760 as a high yield black toner, and Brother's DR730 and TN760 bundle page lists a separate drum unit, DR-730, for the same printer.

Ink bottles

Tank printers refill built in tanks from a bottle, not a cartridge. Epson's ET-2800 FAQ lists bottle part numbers 522120 black, 522220 yellow, 522320 magenta, and 522420 cyan. Canon's MegaTank ink types article lists bottle numbers such as GI-20, GI-21, and GI-290 for the PIXMA G series and MAXIFY GX series.

Frequently asked questions

How can I tell without opening the printer?

The model name is usually the clue. EcoTank and MegaTank models take ink bottles, LaserJet models and Brother's drum and toner models take toner, and the rest of the printers listed on this site take ink cartridges. Open the model page for the exact number.

Is the toner drum the same part as the toner?

No. Brother's DR730 and TN760 bundle page lists the toner and the drum as two separate numbers, TN-760 and DR-730, for the same printer.

Does a tank printer still have a part number?

Yes, but it is a bottle number, not a cartridge number. Epson's ET-2800 FAQ and Canon's MegaTank ink types article both print bottle numbers for their tank printers, not cartridge numbers.
