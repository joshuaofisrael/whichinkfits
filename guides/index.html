Guides

By Which Ink Fits · Last verified 21 Nov 2026

Four guides. Each one answers a different question, and each one links the manufacturer page it quotes.

Use them in order. Read the name on the front of the printer, then open that model. If the box says 67 and 67XL, the yield guide has the page counts HP prints for those two sizes, and the DeskJet 2700 hub is where those counts sit for the twelve models that share them. If the number on the cartridge does not match a US listing, the region guide shows the other columns HP prints. If the supply is a bottle or a toner, the ink-or-toner guide says so before you land on a cartridge page.

  • Find the model number

    Where the name sits on the printer, and where the cartridge number sits on the box.

  • Ink, toner, or bottle?

    DeskJet and PIXMA cartridges, LaserJet and Brother toner, EcoTank and MegaTank bottles.

  • XL vs standard page yields

    The published page counts for HP 67, Epson 212, Brother LC406, and Canon PG-243.

  • HP numbers by country

    When the same DeskJet section prints 67 in one column and 305 or 805 in another.

Not sure which to open

  • The name is on the printer: find the model number.
  • You are not sure it is ink: ink, toner, or bottle.
  • You are choosing a size: XL and standard yields.
  • The number looks wrong for your country: HP numbers by region.

Frequently asked questions

Which guide should I open first?

If the printer is in front of you, start with the model number guide, then open that model. If you already know the cartridge number and you are choosing a standard or an XL, open the yield guide.

Do the guides list third party ink?

No. A number appears on this site only when HP, Canon, Epson, or Brother prints it. The guides quote those pages and do not add a compatible chart from a store.
