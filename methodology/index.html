Sources and methods

Last verified 16 Nov 2026.

What we use

HP's support document Which cartridges work with my HP printer?, plus the HP toner page for the LaserJet Pro M428 and the HP Canada page for the ENVY 6055e. Canon shop and Knowledge Base pages. Epson product, ink, and FAQ pages. Brother product pages for TN760, the DR730 bundle, LC406, and the MFC-J4335DW.

What we leave out

We do not copy a store's compatible ink chart. We do not list a third party number. If a cell on HP's table is ambiguous, that section is skipped. If two HP sections disagree about one model, the page shows both and says so. Page yield is blank when the source does not print one. HP's other region columns are quoted, and they do not yet have their own cartridge pages.

Dates

The date on a page is the day we checked the source, not a refreshed stamp. We change it when the numbers change.

Which Ink Fits lists the ink, toner, and bottle numbers the printer maker publishes for a model, and the printers a number fits. Independent, and not affiliated with HP, Canon, Epson, or Brother. Confirm the number on the maker's page and on your printer.
