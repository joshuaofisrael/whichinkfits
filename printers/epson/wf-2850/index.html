Epson WorkForce WF-2850 ink: which cartridges fit

By Which Ink Fits · Last verified 16 Nov 2026

Official numbers: 212 Black, 212XL Black, 212 Cyan, 212XL Cyan, 212 Magenta, 212XL Magenta, 212 Yellow, 212XL Yellow.

The table is the manufacturer's list for this model. Yield is filled in only when that source prints a page yield.

Epson's XP-4105 page prints ISO replacement yields for these cartridge numbers: 212 black 150 pages and 212 color 130 pages; 212XL black 500 pages and 212XL color 350 pages. The yield is for the cartridge. Epson's part number page groups XP-4100, XP-4105, WF-2830, and WF-2850 on one table.

Frequently asked questions

Which cartridges fit the Epson WorkForce WF-2850?

The manufacturer lists 212 Black, 212XL Black, 212 Cyan, 212XL Cyan, 212 Magenta, 212XL Magenta, 212 Yellow, 212XL Yellow. Confirm the number on the cartridge and on the manufacturer page linked below.

How do I find the model number?

Read the model name on the front of the printer. The cartridge, toner, or bottle number is printed on the supply and on the box. This page repeats the numbers the manufacturer publishes. It does not replace the label on your printer.

What is the difference between a standard supply and a high yield supply?

Where the maker lists a standard number and a high yield number for the same color, both are on this page. Canon says a PIXMA TR4720 can hold one standard cartridge and one XL cartridge at the same time. HP Canada's ENVY 6055e page says a high yield black cartridge can be installed with a standard color cartridge. Do not assume that mix is true for every printer. Read the maker's page for your model.

Does the manufacturer support third party ink or toner?

Epson's XP-4105 page says the printer is designed for use with Epson cartridges only, not third party cartridges or ink, and that other brands may not function properly even if they are described as compatible. Epson's bottle pages say use of non genuine ink could cause damage not covered under the limited warranty. Which Ink Fits does not recommend either way.
