Canon PIXMA G3270 ink bottles: which supplies fit

By Which Ink Fits · Last verified 16 Nov 2026

Official numbers: GI-21 Black, GI-21 Cyan, GI-21 Magenta, GI-21 Yellow.

This model takes ink bottles, not a cartridge. Yield is filled in only when that source prints a page yield.

Canon prints bottle volume, not a per bottle page yield: GI-21 black 135.0 ml pigment, and cyan, magenta, and yellow 70.0 ml dye each.

Canon's G3270 shop page prints a full bottle set yield in default mode: up to 6,000 black and white pages and up to 7,700 color pages. That figure is for the set, not for one bottle. The refill article also names G3272 for the same GI-21 bottles. G3272 does not have its own page here.

Frequently asked questions

Which ink bottles fit the Canon PIXMA G3270?

The manufacturer lists GI-21 Black, GI-21 Cyan, GI-21 Magenta, GI-21 Yellow. These are bottles for the tanks, not cartridges. Confirm the number on the bottle and on the manufacturer page linked below.

How do I find the model number?

Read the model name on the front of the printer. The cartridge, toner, or bottle number is printed on the supply and on the box. This page repeats the numbers the manufacturer publishes. It does not replace the label on your printer.

What is the difference between a standard supply and a high yield supply?

Bottles are not cartridges. Fill each tank from the bottle color the maker names. A high yield bottle, where the maker lists one, is a larger bottle of the same color, not a different printer.

Does the manufacturer support third party ink or toner?

Canon says genuine Canon inks provide peak performance that is specifically designed for compatible Canon printers. Which Ink Fits does not recommend for or against other ink.
