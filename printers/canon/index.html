Frequently asked questions

Which Canon model should I open?

Match the name on the front of the printer to one of the 43 models. A series hub lists the models taken from that section of the manufacturer pages.

Which cartridge number should I use?

Open the model page and read the table. The numbers are the ones Canon prints, last checked 16 Nov 2026. We do not copy a store's compatible ink chart.

Which Ink Fits lists the ink, toner, and bottle numbers the printer maker publishes for a model, and the printers a number fits. Independent, and not affiliated with HP, Canon, Epson, or Brother. Confirm the number on the maker's page and on your printer.
