Standard and high yield on this series

HP's compatibility document puts twelve DeskJet models in this one section, from the 2710 through the 2755e. On the North America column, every model lists the same four rows: 67 and 67XL black, and 67 and 67XL tri-color. The model pages repeat that table. They do not each have a different yield, so the page counts live here and in the XL and standard yield guide, not copied onto every model.

HP's own cartridge specifications, opened 21 Nov 2026, print the approximate yields. 67 black (3YM56AN) is about 120 pages and 67XL black (3YM57AN) is about 240. 67 tri-color (3YM55AN) is about 100 pages and 67XL tri-color (3YM58AN) is about 200. Each footnote says the test printer was the HP ENVY 6000 All-in-One series, not a DeskJet 2700, and that the average uses ISO/IEC 24711 or HP's own method, printing continuously. Actual yield varies with what is on the page. The compatibility document still does not print these counts.

The other columns are different numbers

The same HP row prints 305 and 305XL for Europe, the Middle East, and Africa, and 805 and 805XL for China, India, and Indonesia. Those are not nicknames for 67. A cartridge bought for a US DeskJet 2755e is the 67 family. Why HP numbers differ by country.

Frequently asked questions

Which model should I open?

Match the name on the front of the printer to one of the 12 models below. The model page has the supply table.

Do these models use the same numbers?

On the manufacturer lists checked 16 Nov 2026, every model linked here shows the same numbers: 67, 67XL.

What page yield does HP print for the ink on the DeskJet 2700 series?

HP's cartridge specifications, checked 21 Nov 2026, say about 120 pages for 67 black, about 240 for 67XL black, about 100 for 67 tri-color, and about 200 for 67XL tri-color. The footnotes say the test printer was the ENVY 6000 series, not this DeskJet, and that the figure is an approximate average.

Do the twelve models use different cartridges?

Not on HP's North America column for this section. 2710, 2720, 2721, 2722, 2723, 2724, 2732, 2742e, 2752, 2752e, 2755, and 2755e all list 67 and 67XL black and tri-color. Open the model only to match the name on the printer.
