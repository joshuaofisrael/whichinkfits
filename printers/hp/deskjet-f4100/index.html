What is distinct about this series

The DeskJet F4100 All-in-One hub covers ten named models (F4135 through F4194). HP's section for this family is a bullet list, not a region table. On the list checked 16 Nov 2026, every model shows the same five numbers: 21 and 54 Black, 57 and 22 Tri-color, and 58 Photo.

Two black numbers and two tri-color numbers on one printer section means HP prints more than one official option per color. The model page table lists each row. It does not tell you to install every number at once. Confirm the number on the cartridge you have, then on HP's page.

This is not the DeskJet 1200 Series. That newer hub uses family 67 on its US column and has a multi-region table. F4100 keeps the older 21 / 54 / 57 / 22 / 58 set and has no region columns in the extract we use.

How to match your F41xx label

Read the full model on the front of the printer (for example F4180 or F4194), then open that model card below. The series name F4100 alone is the hub. The supply table lives on the model page.

Frequently asked questions

Which model should I open?

Match the name on the front of the printer to one of the 10 models below. The model page has the supply table.

Do these models use the same numbers?

On the manufacturer lists checked 16 Nov 2026, every model linked here shows the same numbers: 21, 54, 57, 22, 58.

Why are there two black numbers and two tri-color numbers?

HP's DeskJet F4100 All-in-One section lists 21 and 54 for black, and 57 and 22 for tri-color, plus 58 photo. Each number is an official HP row. Match the number on your cartridge; do not assume every row installs together.

Is this the same ink as the DeskJet 1200 Series?

No. The DeskJet 1200 Series US column lists HP 67. The F4100 section lists 21, 54, 57, 22, and 58. Open the hub that matches the name on your printer.
