What is distinct about this series

The DeskJet 1200 Series hub covers three named models: DeskJet 1210, DeskJet 1212, and DeskJet 1255. On HP's US column checked 16 Nov 2026, every one of those models lists only cartridge family 67 (black and tri-color). There is no photo cartridge and no second black number on that column.

That is different from older DeskJet All-in-One hubs such as the DeskJet F4100 All-in-One, where HP lists several black, tri-color, and photo numbers on the same section.

What HP prints by region for this series

HP's cartridge document puts the DeskJet 1200 Series in a region table. North America and Asia Pacific (excluding India, China, Japan, and Indonesia) list HP 67 Black and HP 67 Tri-color. Other columns print different numbers for the same printers.

Frequently asked questions

Which model should I open?

Match the name on the front of the printer to one of the 3 models below. The model page has the supply table.

Do these models use the same numbers?

On the manufacturer lists checked 16 Nov 2026, every model linked here shows the same numbers: 67.

Is HP 67 the only US number for the DeskJet 1200 Series?

On HP's North America and Asia Pacific column for this series, yes: HP 67 Black and HP 67 Tri-color. Other region columns on the same table print 123, 305, or 805 instead.

Does this series list a high yield 67XL on HP's table?

The DeskJet 1200 Series section we extracted lists 67 Black and 67 Tri-color for the US column. Open a model page and the HP 67 cartridge page for the full family, including any high yield rows HP prints elsewhere.
