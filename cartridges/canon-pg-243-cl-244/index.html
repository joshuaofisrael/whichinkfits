What Canon printed for a page count

On 21 Nov 2026 the PIXMA TS302a support page printed a page yield row for these cartridges: PG-243 black 100 pages and CL-244 color 100 pages. The same row prints the fill: PG-243 black 5.6 ml and CL-244 color 6.2 ml. It does not print an XL of either number. It does print a larger pair on the next line, PG-245 and CL-246, at 180 black and 180 color pages for the standard size and 300 and 300 for the XL.

That 100 is a page count for the cartridge number. The TS302a cell does not say ISO. Canon's separate page, How Page Yields Are Measured, says the mixed text and graphics document is declared to ISO/IEC 24711, from continuous printing on plain paper. This page does not paste the word ISO onto the 100, because the TS302a cell itself does not.

There is no PG-243XL on that spec

A reader looking for an XL of PG-243 will not find one in this row. The step up Canon prints is a different number. PG-245 standard is 180 pages against PG-243's 100, and PG-245XL is 300. Those are not a high yield fill of PG-243. The five MG printers below are the models whose older Canon article listed PG-243 beside PG-245. Other PIXMA models on our PG-245 page are listed for PG-245 only.

The same TS302a row prints PG-245 standard at 8.9 ml. The MG series article we saved on 16 Nov 2026 printed 8.0 ml for PG-245, and 5.6 ml and 6.2 ml for PG-243 and CL-244. The 5.6 and 6.2 still match. The 8.0 and the 8.9 do not. That Knowledge Base article did not load on 21 Nov 2026 (the page returned an error), so this recheck does not overwrite the PG-245 volume on the other page. It only records the disagreement.

The printer list is still the 16 Nov list

Five printers are linked below, from the PG-245 value pack and the MG series article, both checked 16 Nov 2026: MG2420, MG2920, MG2924, MG2520, and MG2922. The value pack names the first three. The article names all five. This recheck did not add or drop one of those five, because the article would not open.

The TS302a support page is a different fact. It is the page that prints the 100 page yield, and it names PG-243 and CL-244 as inks for the TS302a. There is no TS302a model page here yet. Do not read the five links as a complete list of every Canon printer that can hold this cartridge.

Cost per page is the price you pay divided by that 100. Canon's TS302a spec does not print a shelf price, so this page does not invent one. The same arithmetic, for HP, Epson, and Brother as well as this Canon pair, is in the yield guide.

Frequently asked questions

Which printers use Canon PG-243 and CL-244?

5 PIXMA MG models are linked on this page, from Canon's value pack and MG series article checked 16 Nov 2026. The TS302a support page also names these cartridges. TS302a is not one of the five. The Knowledge Base article did not load on 21 Nov 2026, so those five names were not re-confirmed that day.

What page yield does Canon print?

The PIXMA TS302a support page, opened 21 Nov 2026, prints 100 black pages for PG-243 and 100 color pages for CL-244, and fill volumes of 5.6 ml and 6.2 ml. The cell does not say ISO. Canon's measurement page says the mixed-document method follows ISO/IEC 24711.

Is there an XL of PG-243?

Not on the TS302a spec. That page prints PG-245 and CL-246 as separate numbers, at 180 pages standard and 300 pages XL for black and for color. It does not print a PG-243XL or a CL-244XL.

How do I find the model number?

Read the model name on the front of the printer. The cartridge number is printed on the supply and on the box. This page repeats the numbers Canon publishes. It does not replace the label on your printer.
