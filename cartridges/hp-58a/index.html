Frequently asked questions

How do I find the model number?

Read the model name on the front of the printer. The cartridge, toner, or bottle number is printed on the supply and on the box. This page repeats the numbers the manufacturer publishes. It does not replace the label on your printer.

What is the difference between a standard supply and a high yield supply?

A high yield toner holds more toner than the standard cartridge. Install only the number the maker lists for your model. This page does not guess that a standard and a high yield toner are interchangeable unless the maker lists both.

Does the manufacturer support third party ink or toner?

HP Canada's ENVY 6055e ink page says only the original HP cartridges it lists can provide the results the printer was engineered to deliver. HP's cartridge table tells you the number. It does not, on the pages we used, tell you to buy or avoid other ink. Which Ink Fits does not recommend either way.
