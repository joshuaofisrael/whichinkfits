What HP prints for a page yield

HP's compatibility document, the source for the printer list, does not print a page yield for 932. The yield comes from HP's own cartridge specification. HP's South Africa product page for the HP 932 Black Original Ink Cartridge, part CN057AE, lists a black and white yield of about 400 pages. The footnote says the test printer was the HP Officejet 6700 Premium e-All-in-One, and that the figure is an approximate average based on ISO/IEC 24711 or HP testing methodology and continuous printing. Actual yield, HP says, varies considerably with the content of the pages. The same specification lists an 8.5 ml fill, pigment-based ink, a 12 picoliter drop, and 1,088 nozzles. Checked 20 Nov 2026.

932XL is on a different HP page

HP's US shop page for the 932XL High Yield Black cartridge, product CN053AN, lists about 1,000 pages. Its footnote names the same Officejet 6700 Premium test printer and the same ISO/IEC 24711 wording, and it points to hp.com/go/learnaboutsupplies for the method. The compatible printers named on that shop page, when we read it on 20 Nov 2026, include the Officejet 6600 e-All-in-One, the OfficeJet 7110 Wide Format, the OfficeJet 7510, and the OfficeJet 7612.

That shop list is not the six-model list in the table above. The compatibility document we extracted on 16 Nov 2026 has no 932XL row in any section. The OfficeJet 6700 section of that same document does list both 933 and 933XL for cyan, magenta, and yellow, and only 932 (not 932XL) for black. So HP published an XL color number in the compatibility section, and published the 932XL black yield on the cartridge's own shop page instead. This page does not add 932XL to the fit list. Match the number on the cartridge you have, then confirm it on HP's page for your model.

Frequently asked questions

Which printers use HP 932?

6 printers are linked on this page, from HP's compatibility document checked 16 Nov 2026. That document lists 932 Black. It does not list 932XL.

What page yield does HP print for 932?

HP's specification for the 932 Black cartridge CN057AE, checked 20 Nov 2026, says about 400 pages. The footnote says the test printer was the Officejet 6700 Premium e-All-in-One, and that the average is based on ISO/IEC 24711 or HP testing methodology and continuous printing. HP says actual yield varies with page content.

Does 932XL fit the same printers?

Not according to the compatibility document this site uses. That extract has no 932XL row. HP's US shop page for CN053AN rates 932XL at about 1,000 pages and names its own compatible-printer list, which overlaps some of these OfficeJets and is not identical. Confirm the number on your cartridge against HP's page for your model.

How do I find the model number?

Read the model name on the front of the printer. The cartridge number is printed on the supply and on the box. This page repeats the numbers HP publishes. It does not replace the label on your printer.
