Frequently asked questions

How do I find the model number?

Read the model name on the front of the printer. The cartridge, toner, or bottle number is printed on the supply and on the box. This page repeats the numbers the manufacturer publishes. It does not replace the label on your printer.

What is the difference between a standard supply and a high yield supply?

Where the maker lists a standard number and a high yield number for the same color, both are on this page. Canon says a PIXMA TR4720 can hold one standard cartridge and one XL cartridge at the same time. HP Canada's ENVY 6055e page says a high yield black cartridge can be installed with a standard color cartridge. Do not assume that mix is true for every printer. Read the maker's page for your model.

Does the manufacturer support third party ink or toner?

Brother says it strongly recommends Brother Genuine toner, that non genuine toner could cause damage, and that the limited warranty may not cover damage from non Brother supplies. That wording is on the TN760 toner page. Which Ink Fits does not recommend either way.
