Brother LC406 cartridges: which printers use them

By Which Ink Fits · Yields rechecked 20 Nov 2026

7 printers on Brother's lists use LC406. Replacement yields rechecked 20 Nov 2026: about 3,000 black pages for LC406BK, about 6,000 for LC406XLBK, 1,500 for each standard color, and 5,000 for each XL color. Those are approximate ISO/IEC 24711 figures, not the "year of ink" line.

Replacement yields, rechecked 20 Nov 2026

The numbers in the table are the replacement-cartridge yields, not a promise of a year of printing. On the MFC-J4335DW page, Brother's feature line says choose standard yield for up to 3,000 black pages and 1,500 color pages, or high yield for up to 6,000 black and 5,000 color. The disclaimer under that page is stricter about the wording: all of those yields are approximate and are calculated to ISO/IEC 24711, and it states LC406BK at 3,000 pages, each LC406 cyan, magenta, and yellow cartridge at 1,500, LC406XLBK at 6,000, and each LC406XL color cartridge at 5,000.

The MFC-J4345DW XL disclaimer prints the same yields and puts "approx." in front of each count. Brother's help answer on the one-year in-box calculation lists each SKU on its own line and says "up to" those same page counts, still approximate, still ISO/IEC 24711. Checked 20 Nov 2026, those three pages still agree on the replacement numbers. They do not agree on what "a year of ink" means.

A year of ink is a different sum

The MFC-J4335DW disclaimer for "Up to 1-year of ink in-box" uses about 150 pages a month, at 60 percent black and 40 percent color, ISO/IEC 24711, from the four cartridges in the box. The same product page was marked Discontinued when we opened it on 20 Nov 2026. The replacement footnote was still there.

The MFC-J4345DW XL page says the box holds up to 2 years of ink, based on about 300 pages a month at the same 60/40 split. A second footnote on that page says the in-box total is about 7,200 pages, against about 1,800 pages for the MFC-J4335DW, which is the "5,400 more pages" line. That comparison is about cartridges shipped in the box. It is not a second set of LC406 replacement yields.

Brother's help article opens with a one-year claim based on about 300 pages a month, not 150. Further down it names the MFC-J5855DW, MFC-J6555DW, and MFC-J6955DW for that 300-page version, and it says the first install uses extra ink to fill the delivery tubes, once. Those three models are on the printer list below. The J4335DW page still prints 150 pages a month for its own one-year line. Read the footnote on the model you own before you treat "one year" as 3,000 pages.

The printer list itself is the one from Brother's MFC-J4335DW page, the LC406 4-pack page, and the LC406 cyan page, checked 16 Nov 2026. This recheck did not add or drop a model. Ink, toner, or bottle if you are not sure this is the supply in the printer.

Frequently asked questions

Which printers use Brother LC406?

7 printers are linked on this page, from Brother's MFC-J4335DW, LC406 4-pack, and LC406 cyan pages checked 16 Nov 2026. The yield recheck on 20 Nov 2026 did not change that list.

What page yield does Brother print for a replacement LC406?

On the MFC-J4335DW disclaimer checked 20 Nov 2026, the yields are approximate and calculated to ISO/IEC 24711: LC406BK 3,000 pages, LC406 cyan, magenta, and yellow 1,500 each, LC406XLBK 6,000, and LC406XL cyan, magenta, and yellow 5,000 each. The feature line on that page says "up to" those counts. The help article says "up to" as well.

Is one year of ink the same as 3,000 pages?

No. The 3,000 figure is the approximate ISO yield Brother prints for a replacement LC406BK cartridge. The one-year in-box line is a separate calculation. The J4335DW page uses about 150 pages a month for that line. The J4345DW XL page uses about 300 pages a month for a two-year in-box line. The help article's 300-page one-year line names the J5855DW, J6555DW, and J6955DW.

How do I find the model number?

Read the model name on the front of the printer. The cartridge number is printed on the supply and on the box. This page repeats the numbers Brother publishes. It does not replace the label on your printer.
