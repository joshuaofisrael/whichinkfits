Frequently asked questions

Which cartridge page should I open?

Open the number printed on the cartridge, toner, or bottle. The page lists the printers the manufacturer names for that number.

What if my printer is not listed on that page?

It was not on the manufacturer pages checked 16 Nov 2026. Open the printer by model, then confirm the number on the manufacturer page linked there.

Which Ink Fits lists the ink, toner, and bottle numbers the printer maker publishes for a model, and the printers a number fits. Independent, and not affiliated with HP, Canon, Epson, or Brother. Confirm the number on the maker's page and on your printer.
