    Frequently asked questions

    Which page should I open?

    If the printer name is in front of you, open that model. If you have the number on the cartridge, toner, or bottle, open that cartridge page. Both lists come from the manufacturer pages, checked 16 Nov 2026.

    How do I find the model number?

    Read the name on the front of the printer, then search for it above. The cartridge number is on the cartridge and on the box. The guide explains both.

    Do you list third party cartridges?

    No. A number appears here only when HP, Canon, Epson, or Brother prints it for that printer. We do not copy other stores' compatible ink charts.

    What is the difference between a standard cartridge and a high yield cartridge?

    High yield means the manufacturer sells a larger fill of the same color. Both numbers are listed when the manufacturer lists both. Mixing them is described only where the manufacturer says it is allowed.

    Are tank printers different?

    Yes. EcoTank and MegaTank models take ink bottles, not cartridges. Those pages say so in the first paragraph.
