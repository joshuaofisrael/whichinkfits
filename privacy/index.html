Privacy

Last updated 16 Nov 2026. Which Ink Fits (whichinkfits.com) is a trade name of Joshua Israel Ventures LLC, a Florida limited liability company.

Analytics

The launch build uses Cloudflare Web Analytics and Google Analytics 4.

Cloudflare Web Analytics is on every launch page and counts page views. That product does not use cookies and does not track you across other sites.

Google Analytics 4 loads only after you accept the banner. Before that choice, Consent Mode v2 leaves analytics storage and ad storage denied, and the GA4 script is not requested. Accepting turns on analytics storage only. Ad storage stays denied. The site shows no ads. The launch build puts Cookie settings in the footer so you can accept or reject later. Rejecting keeps Google Analytics 4 unloaded.

Email

If you email joshuaofisrael@gmail.com, we use your address and message to reply, and we keep them only as long as we need to.

Advertising

The site shows no ads and has no affiliate links. If that changes, we will update this page first. You would be able to opt out of personalized ads at Google's ad settings and at aboutads.info.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log IP addresses for security.

Children

The site is a general audience site. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.
